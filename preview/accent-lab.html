The accent yellow, on paper

Every row is the same words on the same light background, changing only the colour used for accented text. The yellow button keeps the shipping yellow in every row on purpose — a fill does not have to pass a text contrast ratio, and the question is whether the darker text still reads as the same brand next to it. Ratios are measured in the browser against the background actually painted behind the words.
