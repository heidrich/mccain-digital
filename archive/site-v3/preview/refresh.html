MOCKUP — what changed, and what deliberately did not

1 · The pixel engine left the type. Dotted stems at display size are hard to read and the hover scatter was pulling words apart mid-line. It now runs on every image (they assemble out of a swarm on the way in and re-pixelate under the pointer), on the solid buttons, and it is the logo — a 7×7 mark on the same grid the engine draws on.

2 · The header is a full-bleed stage and the dither field runs there and nowhere else, as asked. Rails and corner marks over it, copy anchored low-left, media plate high-right.

3 · The palette lost its brown. #0d0c0a had red and green above blue, which reads sepia beside a warm yellow. Now #0b0b0c — the logo yellow is the only warm thing on the page, untouched. The green #7a8c00 is the second signal and clears AA on the dark ground on its own (5.23:1).

4 · Nothing was rebuilt. This page loads the real v3.css and the real scripts, so the console, the ⌘K command menu, the FAQ, the service stack, the work track, the scores and the contact form are the ones that already work — not copies that would drift apart from day one. The refresh changes the system, not the machinery.

5 · The motion is checked against numbers, not taste. From the animation-systems skill: micro 120–200 ms, UI 180–260, section entrance 400–800, hero 800–1600 with inner beats, stagger 40–90, fade+rise 12–24 px, hover lift −2 to −6 px, reveal at 20–30 % visible and once. What is in: words rising through a mask on every headline; the hero as a sequence (rule → eyebrow → words → plate → numbers, ~1.1 s); the stage handing over as it leaves, copy faster than plate; the rail reporting the chapter you are in; a scroll cue that retires once obeyed; and hover micros that grow an underline from the side it is read from. Its deletion rule threw out the rest: an animation has to explain hierarchy, confirm an action, guide attention, hold continuity or show craft.

6 · Measured, not judged. Small text on the stage, read from the DELIVERED pixels against the brightest ground cell under each line: coords 7.28:1, fact labels 4.71:1, intro 13.27:1, plate tag 8.39:1. The headline cannot be sampled that way — at leading 0.9 there is no glyph-free strip between its lines, so every percentile in its box is already a letter — so it is measured against the brightest cell the field actually renders: 16.41:1 white, 11.29:1 yellow. A full scroll: 0 long tasks, p90 16.9 ms, p99 17.1, 0 % of frames over 20 ms — with all 43 word timelines running. They did not start out free: on their own they took 9 % of frames over 20 ms and p99 to 32.7. Switching the words off put it back to 0 %, which proved it was them and nothing else, and promoting them fixed it. 390 px: no overflow. Reduced motion gets the finished page, not a faster one.

7 · Still open: real portraits and three cleared client references are the biggest gap before go-live; the command menu's own wordmark does not carry the pixel mark yet (it is injected by menu.js, a live file); and whether the billboard lines get a condensed cut for one more font file.
