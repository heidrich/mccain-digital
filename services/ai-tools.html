(03) — How deep we go

The engine under the use-cases.

Those builds all rest on four capabilities, done properly — each wired into your stack with the guardrails, monitoring and UI that turn a clever demo into something your team relies on every day.

RAG over your own data

Retrieval-augmented generation grounded in your documents, code, tickets or database — with citations, so the model answers from your truth, not its training set. Vector search (pgvector, Qdrant), re-ranking, and a confidence path that lets it say "I don't know".

Cited · abstains when unsure

Autonomous & multi-step agents

Workflows that run themselves — plan, call your real APIs through tools, and check their own work before handing it back. Built with guardrails, retries and a human in the loop where it matters, so automation is reliable rather than a liability.

Guardrails · human approval

MCP tool-servers & integrations

We give models hands: Model Context Protocol servers and function calling into your CRM, ERP, internal APIs and databases — with auth, rate limits and audit logging. The model does real work in your systems, safely.

Auth · rate limits · audit log

Automation that pays for itself

The repetitive work — triage, data entry, summarising, routing, drafting — handed to pipelines (n8n, custom loops, scheduled jobs) that run quietly in the background and give real hours back every week.

Hours back, every week
