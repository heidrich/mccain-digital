(06) — How it goes

Discover the real process.

The process on the org chart and the process in the building are rarely the same one. We build for the second.

01

Discover the real process

We sit with the people who do the work and map how it really runs — the systems, the handoffs, the manual steps. You get a build plan grounded in reality, not in an org chart.

02

Prototype

A clickable prototype of the core flow before we commit to the full build — so you correct course on something you can see and click, not on a 40-page spec nobody finished reading.

03

Build, with weekly demos

We build against your real data and integrations, and show working software every week. No big-bang reveal at the end — you steer the whole way, and there are no surprises.

04

Ship & hand over

Deployed where your data lives — cloud or on-prem — with documented architecture, the source in your repository and a proper handover. Plus 30 days of support after launch.
